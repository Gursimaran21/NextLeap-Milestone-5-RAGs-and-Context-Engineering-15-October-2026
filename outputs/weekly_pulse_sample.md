# Weekly Product Pulse — Groww (Week of 2026-10-12)

**1. Top Themes**
- **Charges & Fees (14 reviews, 35%)** — the largest cluster; users report deductions they did not expect after redeeming or selling.
- **Payments & SIP (8 reviews, 20%)** — failed or self-cancelled SIPs with generic errors and no refund clarity.
- **Withdrawals & Redemption (5 reviews, 13%)** — slow payouts and no visible status after redemption.

**2. What Users Are Saying**
- "Redeemed my mutual fund and got less than the value shown in the app. Nobody told me about exit load." — 2★, Jul
- "The redeem screen shows one amount but the bank credit is less. Very misleading." — 1★, Sep
- "Zero brokerage is great, but the charges section should be clearer." — 4★, Aug

**3. Key Observation**
Fee confusion is the dominant and rising signal, not a one-off. The same complaint recurs
across weeks: the redeem/sell screen shows a gross value, the credited value is lower, and the
reason is not surfaced in the flow. Payments reliability is the second signal, with failed SIPs
reported without an actionable reason.

**4. 3 Action Ideas**
1. Add an inline "expected exit load" line to the redeem confirmation screen.
2. Publish a single in-app "All Charges" page linked from the sell/redeem flow.
3. Return a specific failure reason + auto-retry for failed SIP debits.

**5. Fee Signal**
Detected fee issue: **Exit Load on Mutual Fund Redemption** — 9 matching reviews.
Users do not associate the lower credited amount with exit load.
